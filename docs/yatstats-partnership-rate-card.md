8. **All-Time Next-Level List** — Sponsor a Team banner + Row 5 YaTi promotion **combined** in one package.
9. **Fan communications** — title-branded eligible school-to-fan email/SMS updates, subject to permissions, consent, delivery, and templates.

## Evidence needed before pricing

| Commercial question | Measure | Tracking instrument / note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How many see an eligible ad unit? | Eligible page visits, render opportunities, viewport-confirmed impressions, share-of-voice | Record **viewable** impressions per placement and viewport. A loaded tab is not proof the ad was viewed. |
| What is the size of the actual school audience? | School-level unique sessions, fan subscriptions, distinct eligible recipients, return frequency | Deduplicate across devices and email where possible; clearly report limits. |
| Do they respond to an offer? | Placement clicks and confirmed outbound visits | Use a sponsor-specific campaign/UTM ID and CTA event. |
| Do clicks produce results? | Qualified inquiry rate, appointment submissions and sponsor-reported conversions | Tag conversion source and verify attribution window; no invented conversion rate. |
| Do messages arrive? | Eligible recipients, attempted sends, deliveries, bounces, opt-outs and clicks | For email/SMS, account for consent and deliverability. Opens are optional and unreliable for true human reads. |
| Is bracket advertising actually visible? | Screen width, stats-drawer state, ad-slot in viewport, time visible | **Do not count** views while any panel hides the slot. |
| Is a profile sponsored slot visible? | Player status, selected tab (News/Connect/Stories), responsive unit, viewability | Keep active vs retired profiles segmented. |
| Does Row 5 sell local offers? | Team-year impression, banner exposure, YaTi offer view, CTA click, verified lead | Both units belong to the same package; report separately, sell together. |
| What can we actually fulfill? | Creative specs, ad slot sizes, support workload, frequency and renewal terms | Establish inventory and approval rules before contractual guarantees. |

Illustrative methods, **not promises or an automatic price quote**:

- Delivered ad equivalents = **eligible ad opportunities × actual viewability × purchased share-of-voice**.
- CPM media comparison = **viewable delivered impressions ÷ 1,000 × a comparable verified CPM**.
- Response-value scenario = **attributed clicks × verified qualified-conversion rate × sponsor value per qualified result**.
- Email/SMS correspondence exposure uses **messages delivered**, tracked separately from on-site impressions and not conflated with unique people.
- Proposed package quote should also cover implementation/creative labor, review and fulfillment, customer support, local brand affinity, duration and any documented competitive exclusivity.
- Any *retired profile discount* should appear as a **discount on the same profile package**, not as a weaker set of placements.
- Non-guaranteed or unmeasured inventory must be labeled **forecast / pending validation**.

## Before quoting a sponsor

- [ ] Confirm the exact school/profile/team scope, term, creative rights, messaging approval, and whether the inventory is shared or exclusive.
- [ ] Define each placement's eligible surface and desktop/mobile display behavior.
- [ ] Capture a baseline traffic and viewability period.
- [ ] Assign sponsor destination links, attribution labels and reporting expectations.
- [ ] Approve delivery and makegood terms, if any.
- [ ] Approve price and billing terms separately.

**Business framing:** Advertisers are buying a combination of relevant hometown identity, repeat alumni attention, and direct, attributable responses. Raw school count, stat-row count and hypothetical impressions are not a defensible rate card without verified audience and visibility.
